A closer look

Everything your shop does.
One connected system.

Explore how Digital Khata simplifies everyday tasks, connects your business records and helps you stay in control.

01 Customer accounts

Every customer has a khata. The balance is always right.

Record "You gave" or "You got" in one tap. Digital Khata maintains each running balance, so there is no adding up at night and no confusion about amounts.

  • Separate khatas for customers, suppliers and bank accounts
  • Opening balances, collection dates and a daily collection list
  • Statements for any date range, printable or downloadable as PDF
Customer khata · Running balance
02 Payment reminders

Get your money back faster.

Send a polite reminder with the exact balance and your Easypaisa or JazzCash number. It takes one tap, and your customer receives the right amount.

  • WhatsApp reminders, SMS reminders or direct calls from the khata
  • Collection dates on the dashboard and in morning notifications
Reminders · Faster collections
03 Sales counter

A bill in seconds, at the counter or on your phone.

Tap item tiles or scan a barcode, enter the weight in grams and choose how the customer paid. Any unpaid amount can be added to the customer's khata.

  • Barcode scanning with a phone camera or scanner gun
  • Enter 1200g and calculate it as 1.2 kg at the per-kg rate
  • Cash, bank, Easypaisa or JazzCash, with discounts, tax and part payments
  • Month-wise dues on bills and photo proof from the camera
Sales counter · Quick billing
04 Receipts & stock

Print the bill. Stock updates itself.

Every bill can be printed as an A4 invoice or a 58 mm or 80 mm thermal receipt with your shop name and payment details. Sold items are deducted from stock at the same time.

  • PDF downloads, WhatsApp sharing and paid-bill reprints
  • Units such as kg, dozen, litre and carton, with photos and barcodes
  • Low-stock alerts, stock value and date-wise stock reports
Billing & inventory · Connected records
05 Purchases NEW

Buy from suppliers item by item. Stock and khata update themselves.

Enter the supplier's bill once, including each item's quantity, unit and purchase rate. Digital Khata adds the items to stock, records the supplier's bill and payment in their khata, and updates the cash book.

  • Cash purchases and quick supplier creation
  • Item-wise view of quantities bought, average rate, sold and remaining stock
  • Supplier-wise view of purchases, payments and outstanding balance
  • Supplier bill photos and saved latest purchase rates
Purchases · Supplier management
06 Profit & reports

See your real profit, item by item.

Understand your profit and loss for any period, along with the profit and margin on every item, calculated from your purchases and sales.

  • Item-wise cost, sales, profit, margin and wastage
  • Today's and this month's sales on the owner's dashboard
  • Transaction, payment and staff reports with six-month charts
Reports · Business performance
07 Language & accessibility

Works in your language, even without internet.

Use Digital Khata in English, Urdu, Sindhi or Roman Urdu, and switch whenever you need. If the internet goes down, keep billing; your data uploads when you're back online.

  • Open it in your browser and install it on your home screen like an app
  • One login for up to three businesses or branches
Four languages · Flexible access

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS 10 QUESTIONS
01 Do I need to download an app?

No. Digital Khata opens in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop. You can add it to your home screen and use it like an app.

02 Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can continue making bills and entries while offline. They sync automatically when your internet connection returns.

03 Can I use it in Urdu or Sindhi?

Yes. Digital Khata supports English, Urdu, Sindhi and Roman Urdu. You can switch between languages whenever you need.

04 Can I add my old udhaar from the register?

Yes. When you add a customer or supplier, simply enter their existing balance and continue managing their account digitally.

05 Can my staff use it without seeing my profit?

Yes. Staff can have their own login with assigned permissions. Sensitive information such as purchase prices, cash book and reports can remain restricted to the owner.

06 Can it print bills?

Yes. You can print A4 invoices, 58 mm and 80 mm thermal receipts, save bills as PDF and share them through WhatsApp.

07 Does Digital Khata take payments or hold my money?

No. Digital Khata records payments made through cash, bank, Easypaisa or JazzCash. It does not collect or hold your money.

08 I have more than one shop. Do I need two accounts?

No. One login can manage up to 3 businesses or branches, with separate records for each.

09 What happens after the free trial?

You can choose a suitable plan and continue using Digital Khata. Your existing customers, bills and stock records remain available according to your plan.
